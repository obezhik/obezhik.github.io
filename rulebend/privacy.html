Rulebend — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Rulebend (package com.obezhyk.rulebend) collects, why, and what choices you have.

Who is responsible

Rulebend is developed by Oleh Bezhyk (publishing as “OB Developer”), Ukraine.
Contact: obezhyk.developer@gmail.com

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. The developer runs no servers and does not receive your name, email, location or contacts.
  • Your games, scores, settings and progress are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to measure usage and find crashes. These Google services receive device identifiers and usage data, as described below.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (the current game, best scores, daily challenge streak, missions and stars), your settings (sound, vibration, theme, language) and whether you bought “No Ads” are kept in the app’s private storage. They are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Data processed by third-party services

Advertising — Google AdMob

Rulebend shows full-screen ads between games and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects the device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ad is requested. Personalized ads are shown only if you agree; otherwise ads may be non-personalized.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying “No Ads” removes the ads between games. Rewarded videos stay optional.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Usage statistics — Firebase Analytics

To understand how the game is played and improve its balance, Firebase Analytics records game events (a game started or ended with its mode, score, level and number of moves; a second chance used; a daily challenge completed; an ad shown) together with an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, app version, country and language. Where consent is required, collection starts only after you allow it.

Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report: the stack trace, device model, operating system version, free memory and storage, and an installation ID. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Where consent is required, crash reports are sent only after you allow it.

Game settings — Firebase Remote Config

At start-up the app downloads game settings (for example, how often ads may appear and difficulty values). For this, Google receives a Firebase installation ID and your IP address. No usage or personal data is stored by this request.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

“No Ads” is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app only receives the confirmation that the purchase was made. Payment details never reach the app or the developer.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized ads, usage statistics and crash reports where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interest — showing non-personalized ads that fund the free game, preventing ad fraud, downloading game settings, and crash reports and statistics where consent is not required.
  • Contract — processing the “No Ads” purchase.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your consent at any time: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalization in Android settings.
  • Delete all data stored on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to processing, or complain to your data protection authority. Because the developer does not receive data that identifies you, such requests are fulfilled through Google’s services; write to the email above and we will help.

Children

Rulebend is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

Data transfers and security

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards. Data in transit to Google is encrypted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
